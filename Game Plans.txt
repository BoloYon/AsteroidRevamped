Currnecy Hierarchy
	Astrynite: Current game currencies for upgrades (both lost on death)
	Glintcores: Semipermenant upgrades kept after death (reset on pristeiege)
	Singulite: Gotten by combining glint cores, semi-perm boost in resource drops
	Galaxite: fusing singulite (the pristiege material) permenant boosts
	Cosmonite: Fusing Galaxite, unknown use (for now)
	Univite: fusing Cosmonite, uknown use
	Eternite: fusing univite, unknown use, possibly last currency
