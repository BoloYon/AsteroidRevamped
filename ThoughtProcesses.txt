Collisions of attacks
	Where should it be handled?
		Attacks Only:
			Filter so the attacker doesnt get hit by its own attack
			Know what area it just entered and send a signal to act accordingly
		Reciever Only:
			Filter for attacker again
			Filter another time for an attack not from an ally and act accordingly
		Both:
			Future proof incase I want to add chained, homing, or bouncing/ splitting effects

	Final Verdict:
	 	Handle collisions in both the attack and the receiver, with the receiver having final authority.
			The attack focuses on behavior after contact and early rejection when possible.
			The receiver focuses on accepting/rejecting hits and applying effects.
		This reduces unnecessary processing and keeps advanced attack behaviors easy to add.
